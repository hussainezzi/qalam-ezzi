The master

A lifetime at the qalam.

Mohammad Ezzi is a Dubai-based calligrapher working in the classical Arabic scripts. His work moves between the discipline of the old masters and a warm, contemporary hand.

He teaches the same way he writes — slowly, precisely, and with the student's own hand in mind. Classes run live online, so a student in Toronto and a student in Sharjah learn side by side.
